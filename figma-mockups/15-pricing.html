Frequently Asked Questions
Can I switch plans later? ▾
Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes take effect immediately, and we'll prorate the difference.
What payment methods do you accept? ▾
We accept all major credit cards, MetaMask wallet, and bank transfers for Studio plans.
Is there a free trial for Pro? ▾
Yes! Pro comes with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required to start.
Do I keep my files if I downgrade? ▾
Yes, all your files remain accessible. You just won't be able to upload new ones beyond your plan's storage limit.
