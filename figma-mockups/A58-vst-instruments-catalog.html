Bad Karma Box
This Live Pack allows you to step into the "headquarters" between 2008 and 2012. It's a collection of loops, sounds and clips I've used in all my releases and remixes for many years by now. It's pretty close to a everyday template.
This particular kit is just for you, and was arranged in a way that will make it easy to add your own material and either play with your friends or create a original track. It features a lot of stems and drafts from my albums (under my alias "Shackleton666") lined up in a way that makes them freely combinable... so technically, it's up to you now to write those missing track(s).
